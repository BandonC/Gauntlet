How to read these ratings

Live ratings update after every match, so they depend on play order. Ranked by live Weng-Lin ordinal μ − 3σ (any table size); Glicko-2 and Elo are heads-up only. Final rating is order-independent: a Bradley-Terry fit to one finished heads-up tournament, with a 95% interval against that field.
