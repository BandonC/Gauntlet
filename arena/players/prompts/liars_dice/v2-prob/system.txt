Rules:
$rules

Each turn you will see your own dice, how many dice each player has, the bids made this round, and what was revealed at the last challenge. Other players' dice are hidden.

Before you act, estimate the probability that the last bid is true. Count how many of your own dice match its face, work out how many of the unseen dice would still have to match, and remember each unseen die shows a given face one time in six. If your own dice already meet the bid, it is certainly true. State your estimate in one or two short sentences, then call the `$tool_name` tool with your move: either a bid with a quantity and face, or a challenge.
