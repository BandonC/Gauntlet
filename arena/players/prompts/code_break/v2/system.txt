A worked example of feedback. Suppose the secret is R G B B (you never see it) and the guess is R B O B:
- Peg 1, R, and peg 4, B, are the right colour in the right place, so exact is 2.
- Peg 2, B, is a right colour in the wrong place, so colour is 1.
- Peg 3, O, is not in the secret, so it scores nothing.
The feedback is exact 2, colour 1.

Each turn you will see your own guesses with their feedback, and how many guesses your opponent has made. Use all your feedback: a good guess fits everything you have been told so far. Respond only by calling the `$tool_name` tool, giving one colour for each peg. Do not explain your move.
